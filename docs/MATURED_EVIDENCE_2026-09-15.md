# First completed paper outcomes

As of September 15, 2026, two frozen baseline paper cohorts have completed
their full evaluation windows. Both delivered positive gross returns and
outperformed SPY over the same entry-to-exit periods.

| Completed cohort | Gross return | Matched SPY return | Outperformance |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | +3.12% | -1.31% | +4.43 percentage points |
| B | +3.29% | -2.07% | +5.35 percentage points |

These are separate overlapping paper outcomes, not a compounded account
return. The cohorts use two baseline model vintages, so they are not repeated
independent tests of one unchanged model. No candidate-model performance is
inferred from them.

The review verified frozen forecast integrity, exact exchange-session maturity
and matched benchmark opening-price windows. Historical calendar discrepancies
were resolved against the exact session schedule. Returns use adjusted daily
opening prices from Yahoo Finance, before transaction costs; they are not
broker fills and have not received independent-source price confirmation.

The early results are encouraging, but two overlapping observations cannot
establish persistent effectiveness, statistical significance or readiness for
capital deployment. Annualizing these outcomes would be misleading. In-progress
cohorts remain excluded until their final evaluation opens are observed.

This update reports completed paper evidence only. It publishes no private
holdings, model artifacts, scores, account information or trading rules.

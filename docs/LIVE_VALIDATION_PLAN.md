# Live Validation and Scaling Plan

The live process evaluates whether the complete system behaves as historical
testing assumed. Short-term profitability is secondary.

## Phase 1: live-forward monitoring

Run immutable paper decisions for at least two calendar months and record:

- whether signals are generated on schedule from the frozen model;
- actual observable prices versus assumed execution prices;
- slippage, turnover, skipped decisions and data failures;
- drawdown, severe losses, concentration and benchmark-relative behaviour;
- every manual intervention and its counterfactual model action; and
- independent reconstruction of positions, cash and returns.

Two months can reveal operational disagreement and allow several outcomes to
mature. It cannot establish long-run expected return.

## Phase 2: approximately $4,000 pilot

After monitoring passes, use a small amount of real capital to observe effects
that paper accounting cannot reproduce:

- fills, partial fills and order rejection;
- spread and realized slippage;
- fractional-share and position-rounding behaviour;
- fees, cash drag and brokerage timing;
- corporate-action handling; and
- operator compliance with the frozen decision rule.

## Phase 3: gradual scaling

Capital can increase only when:

- live signals reproduce the frozen research pipeline;
- actual and assumed execution reconcile within predeclared tolerances;
- slippage and turnover remain within tested stress ranges;
- drawdown and tail losses reveal no unmodelled risk;
- interventions are absent or limited to declared safety rules; and
- accounting can be reproduced independently.

Repeat the same audit at every capital level. Do not scale solely because the
monitor or pilot made money.
